Fake Out is a +3 priority move that always causes a flinch on the first turn the user is out (Thanks to Scrappy, Kangaskhan can hit Ghost types with it before Mega Evolving!). By using Fake Out on Gengar, you deny its Hypnosis while your ally gets a free attack.
