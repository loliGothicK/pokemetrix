options:

  - "Draws the ally's single-target moves to the user."
  - "Nullifies the opponents' spread moves."
  - "Nullifies all moves targeting the user."
correctAnswer: "Draws the opponents' single-target moves to the user."
---

Follow Me draws all single-target moves used by the opponents to the user. It cannot redirect spread moves (such as Earthquake or Heat Wave). It is a crucial support move in Double Battles to protect allies.
