question: "Which type does not take continuous HP damage from Hail?"
options:
  - "Ice type"
  - "Water type"
  - "Snow type"
  - "Flying type"
correctAnswer: "Ice type"
---

During Hail, Ice-type Pokémon do not take continuous HP damage. Additionally, in Snow, the physical Defense of Ice-type Pokémon is multiplied by 1.5. Water- and Flying-types take damage as usual under Hail.
