【Explanation】
The correct answer is "Lowers targets' Speed stats by 1 stage.".

Icy Wind is a spread move that deals damage and is guaranteed to lower the Speed of all targets hit by one stage. This makes it a very useful tool for speed control. Unlike other Ice-type attacks like Ice Beam or Blizzard, it does not have a chance to freeze the opponent. Moves like Cotton Spore lower Speed by two stages, whereas Icy Wind only lowers it by one. Additionally, there is no mechanic where it deals double damage to already frozen targets.
