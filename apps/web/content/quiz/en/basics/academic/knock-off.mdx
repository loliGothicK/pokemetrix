【Explanation】
The correct answer is "This move's power is boosted by 50% if the target is holding an item. Causes the target to lose its held item.".

Knock Off is a widely used move because it disarms the target by dropping their held item, and it deals 50% more damage if it successfully removes an item. However, it does not steal the item for the user; that is the effect of Thief or Covet. The move does not fail if the target has no item (unlike Poltergeist), and it does not have an increased critical hit ratio or guarantee a critical hit.
