[Explanation]
The Choice Scarf boosts the holder's Speed stat by 1.5x but restricts them to exclusively using the first move they select until they switch out.
Boosting the power of physical moves by 1.5x is the effect of the Choice Band. There are no items that increase damage taken by 1.5x in exchange for Speed, nor any that double Speed while restricting the holder to status moves.
