question: What is the primary effect of the status move Thunder Wave?
options:
  - It paralyzes the target.
  - It deals damage and paralyzes the target.
  - It lowers the target's Speed by two stages.
  - It confuses the target.
correctAnswerIndex: 0
reviewed: true
---
【Explanation】
The correct answer is "It paralyzes the target."

Thunder Wave is a basic status move that inflicts paralysis on the target. It does not deal direct damage, unlike attacks such as Nuzzle or Zap Cannon. While paralysis does halve the Pokémon's Speed, the move itself does not lower stat stages like Cotton Spore does. Furthermore, it causes paralysis, not confusion, which is an effect of moves like Confuse Ray.
