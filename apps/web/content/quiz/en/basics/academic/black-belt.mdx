[Explanation]
The Black Belt boosts the power of the holder's Fighting-type moves by 1.2x.
Boosting super effective moves by 1.2x is the effect of the Expert Belt. Boosting all physical moves by 1.1x is the effect of the Muscle Band. Halving the damage taken from a super effective Fighting-type move is the effect of the Chople Berry.
