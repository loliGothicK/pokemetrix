[Explanation]
"U-turn" is a pivoting move that allows the user to deal damage and then switch out for another Pokémon in the party.
Unlike "Roar" or "Dragon Tail", it does not force the opponent to switch. It allows the user to switch regardless of who moves first, and it does not inflict any status conditions like confusion.
