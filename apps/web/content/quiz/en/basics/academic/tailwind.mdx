【Explanation】
The correct answer is "Doubles the Speed of all Pokémon on the user's side for 4 turns."

"Tailwind" is a status condition that doubles the Speed of the user and its allies for 4 turns, including the turn it is set up.
It is easy to confuse this with "Trick Room", weather, or terrain effects which typically last for 5 turns.
