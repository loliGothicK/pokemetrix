question: "How much continuous HP damage do you take each turn from binding moves like Wrap?"
options:
  - "1/8 of max HP"
  - "1/16 of max HP"
  - "1/6 of max HP"
  - "1/4 of max HP"
correctAnswer: "1/8 of max HP"
---

Continuous damage from binding moves (such as Wrap, Fire Spin, etc.) is 1/8 of the target's max HP. However, if the Pokémon using the binding move is holding a Binding Band, the damage becomes 1/6.
