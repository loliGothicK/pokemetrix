  - Boosts the power of Fighting-type moves by 1.2x.
  - Boosts the power of all moves by 1.1x.
  - 'Boosts the power of moves by 1.3x, but takes recoil damage after attacking.'
correctAnswer: Boosts the power of super effective moves by 1.2x.

[Explanation]
The Expert Belt boosts the power of the holder's moves by 1.2x only when they are super effective against the target.
Boosting Fighting-type moves by 1.2x is the effect of the Black Belt. Boosting all physical or special moves by 1.1x relates to the Muscle Band or Wise Glasses. Boosting all moves by 1.3x at the cost of recoil damage is the effect of the Life Orb.
