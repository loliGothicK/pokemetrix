【Explanation】
The correct answer is "For 5 turns, slower Pokémon move first within their priority bracket."

"Trick Room" is a field condition that reverses the turn order based on Speed, allowing Pokémon with lower Speed stats to move before those with higher Speed stats for 5 turns (including the turn it is set).
It only reverses the action order based on Speed; it does not reverse the priority brackets of priority moves like "Quick Attack". Also, swapping Attack and Defense is the effect of "Power Trick", and swapping Defense and Special Defense is the effect of "Wonder Room".
