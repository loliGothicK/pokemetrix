【Explanation】
The correct answer is "It strikes first and makes the target flinch, but can only be selected on the user's first turn in battle."

Fake Out has a high priority (+3) and a 100% chance to make the target flinch. As of Generation 9, it can only be selected on the very first turn the user enters the battlefield; on subsequent turns, the move cannot even be chosen. It does not cause recoil damage to the user. It will hit regardless of whether the opponent selected an attack or a status move, unlike Sucker Punch which fails if the opponent isn't attacking. Finally, Fake Out does not remove held items, which is the effect of Knock Off.
