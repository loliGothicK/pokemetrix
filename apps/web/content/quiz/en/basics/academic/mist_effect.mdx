options:
  - "Prevents stat reductions for all allies for 5 turns"
  - "Prevents your stat reductions for 1 turn"
  - "Nullifies all of the opponent's status moves"
  - "Makes you unaffected by weather"
correctAnswer: "Prevents stat reductions for all allies for 5 turns"
---

Mist prevents the stats of allies from being lowered by the opponent's moves or abilities for 5 turns, including the turn it was used. However, it does not prevent stat reductions caused by your own moves (such as the Defense and Special Defense drops from Close Combat).
