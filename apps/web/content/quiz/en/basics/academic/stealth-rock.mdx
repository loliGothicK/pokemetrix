【Explanation】
The correct answer is "Damages Pokémon switching in for 1/8 of their max HP, altered by their weakness or resistance to Rock."

"Stealth Rock" sets an entry hazard around the opponent's field that damages Pokémon switching in. Crucially, the amount of damage is calculated based on the target's type effectiveness against Rock-type moves (1/8 for neutral).
It does not deal fixed damage regardless of type. Additionally, lowering the Speed of switching Pokémon is the effect of "Sticky Web", and inflicting the Poison status is the effect of "Toxic Spikes".
