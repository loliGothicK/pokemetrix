question: "You use Protect successfully with Clefable on Turn 1. On Turn 2, you select Protect again with Clefable. What is the chance that it will succeed?"
options:

  - "50%"
  - "0%"
  - "100%"
correctAnswer: "33%"

Using Protect (or similar moves like Detect, Spiky Shield, King's Shield) consecutively reduces its success rate to roughly 33% (1/3) on the second attempt. A third consecutive attempt has an 11% (1/9) chance.
