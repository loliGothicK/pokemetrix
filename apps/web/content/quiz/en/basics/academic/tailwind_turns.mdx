question: "When Tailwind is used, how many turns does its effect last?"
options:
  - "4 turns including the turn it is used"
  - "3 turns including the turn it is used"
  - "5 turns including the turn it is used"
  - "2 turns including the turn it is used"
correctAnswer: "4 turns including the turn it is used"
---
Tailwind doubles the Speed of you and your allies for 4 turns, including the turn it was used. Because the effect wears off after the end of the 4th turn, practically speaking there are "3 turns starting from the turn after the move is used" where you receive the benefit.
