correctAnswer: "1/16 of maximum HP"

During a sandstorm, Pokemon that are not Rock, Steel, or Ground types take damage equal to 1/16 of their maximum HP each turn. Additionally, Rock-type Pokemon receive a bonus where their Special Defense is multiplied by 1.5.
