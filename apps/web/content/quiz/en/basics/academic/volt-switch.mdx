[Explanation]
"Volt Switch" is an Electric-type special pivoting move that allows the user to switch out to a party Pokémon immediately after dealing damage.
However, if the target is a Ground-type or has an ability that completely nullifies the damage, the move fails and the user will not switch. It also does not have a guaranteed paralysis effect, nor does it force the opponent to switch.
