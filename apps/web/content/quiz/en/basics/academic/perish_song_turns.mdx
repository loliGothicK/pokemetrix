  - "After 5 counts (5 turns after the move is used)"
  - "Faints immediately"
correctAnswer: "After 3 counts (3 turns after the move is used)"
---

Pokemon affected by Perish Song are given a count of 3, which decreases by 1 at the end of each turn. They faint at the end of the turn when the count reaches 0. If they switch out, the count is carried over.
