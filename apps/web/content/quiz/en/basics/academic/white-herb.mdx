[Explanation]
The White Herb restores any lowered stat stages to normal once, being consumed in the process. It is useful for negating the stat drops from moves like Close Combat or Abilities like Intimidate.
Curing Taunt and similar volatile conditions is the effect of the Mental Herb. Curing non-volatile status conditions is the effect of the Lum Berry. There is no item that natively increases the amount of stat boosts.
