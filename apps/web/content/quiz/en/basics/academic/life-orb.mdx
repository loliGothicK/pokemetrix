[Explanation]
The Life Orb boosts the power of the holder's damaging moves by 1.3x, but the holder takes recoil damage equal to 1/10 of its maximum HP after each successful attack.
Boosting physical moves by 1.1x is the effect of the Muscle Band. Boosting moves by 1.5x while restricting move choice is the effect of Choice Band or Choice Specs. Boosting super effective moves by 1.2x is the effect of the Expert Belt.
