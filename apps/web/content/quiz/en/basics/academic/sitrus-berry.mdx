[Explanation]
The Sitrus Berry restores 1/4 of the holder's maximum HP when their HP falls to 50% or less.
Restoring HP when HP falls to 1/4 or less is the effect of confusion berries like the Figy Berry. Restoring 1/16 of max HP at the end of every turn is the effect of Leftovers.
