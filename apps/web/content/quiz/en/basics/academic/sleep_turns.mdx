options:
  - "Randomly wakes up in 2-5 turns"
  - "Guaranteed to wake up after 3 turns"
  - "Randomly wakes up in 1-3 turns"
  - "Cannot wake up on its own"
correctAnswer: "Randomly wakes up in 2-5 turns"
---
Sleep randomly wears off in 2 to 5 turns (the turn the Pokemon falls asleep is not counted). When falling asleep from Rest, the Pokemon is guaranteed to wake up after 2 turns. It is also possible to cure it immediately using items such as a Chesto Berry.
