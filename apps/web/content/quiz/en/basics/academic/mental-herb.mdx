[Explanation]
The Mental Herb cures the holder of infatuation, Taunt, Encore, Torment, Disable, and Heal Block once, allowing them to execute their moves freely.
Curing non-volatile status conditions is the effect of the Lum Berry. Restoring lowered stat stages is the effect of the White Herb.
