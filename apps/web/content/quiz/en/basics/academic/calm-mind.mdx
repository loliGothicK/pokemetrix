"Calm Mind" is a Psychic-type status move that raises the user's Special Attack and Special Defense by 1 stage each.
Raising Special Attack and Speed by 1 stage is similar to other setup moves, raising Special Attack by 2 stages is the effect of "Nasty Plot", and raising Special Defense by 2 stages is the effect of "Amnesia".
