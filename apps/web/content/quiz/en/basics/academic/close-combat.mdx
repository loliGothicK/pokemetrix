[Explanation]
"Close Combat" is a high-power move that comes with the drawback of lowering the user's Defense and Special Defense by 1 stage each after use.
Be careful not to confuse it with "Superpower" (which lowers Attack and Defense) or recoil moves like "Flare Blitz" that cause the user to take damage based on what was dealt to the target.
