[Explanation]
The Lum Berry cures the holder of any non-volatile status condition (such as poison, paralysis, burn, freeze, or sleep) as well as confusion, consuming the item in the process.
Restoring HP is the effect of berries like the Sitrus Berry. Restoring lowered stats is the effect of the White Herb. Curing only sleep is the specific effect of the Chesto Berry.
