question: "Which stat does the Intimidate ability lower?"
options:
  - "Attack only"
  - "Attack and Defense"
  - "Attack and Special Attack"
  - "Speed only"
correctAnswer: "Attack only"
---

Intimidate is an ability that lowers the opponent's Attack stat by one stage. It does not affect Defense, Special Attack, Special Defense, or Speed. In a Double Battle, it lowers the Attack of both opponents on the field by one stage each.
