【Explanation】
The correct answer is "The user protects itself from incoming moves for the turn. With each consecutive use this move's chance of success becomes 1/3 of what it was before.".

Protect is a priority move that prevents most attacks from affecting the user during that turn, but using it on consecutive turns decreases its chance of success. It does not cause damage to attackers making contact (unlike Spiky Shield or Baneful Bunker), nor does it lower any stats (unlike King's Shield, which lowers the attacker's stats). Furthermore, it does not completely grant absolute invulnerability, as certain moves like Feint or Phantom Force can bypass Protect entirely.
