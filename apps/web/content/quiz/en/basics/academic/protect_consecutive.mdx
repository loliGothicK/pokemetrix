question: "When using Protect for 2 consecutive turns, what is the success rate of the second use?"
options:
  - "About 33% (1/3)"
  - "About 50% (1/2)"
  - "0% (Guaranteed to fail)"
  - "100% (Guaranteed to succeed)"
correctAnswer: "About 33% (1/3)"
---
Protective moves such as Protect, Detect, and King's Shield have their success rate reduced to about 1/3 (33%) if they were successfully used on the previous turn. If used for 3 consecutive turns, it becomes about 1/9 (11%). Moves that bypass protection like Feint will nullify Protect.
