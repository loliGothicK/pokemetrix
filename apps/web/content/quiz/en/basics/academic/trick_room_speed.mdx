question: "While Trick Room is active, what happens to the turn order?"
options:
  - "Pokemon with lower Speed move first"
  - "Pokemon with higher Speed move first (no change)"
  - "All Pokemon move at the same time"
  - "The Pokemon that used Trick Room always moves first"
correctAnswer: "Pokemon with lower Speed move first"
---
While Trick Room is active, the turn order is reversed, and Pokemon with lower Speed move first. However, move priority (such as positive priority moves) takes precedence over Trick Room.
