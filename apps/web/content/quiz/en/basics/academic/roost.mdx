[Explanation]
"Roost" restores up to half of the user's maximum HP. If used by a Flying-type Pokémon, it temporarily loses its Flying type until the end of that turn.
Unlike "Synthesis" or "Morning Sun", the recovery amount is not affected by weather. The loss of the Flying type is only for the duration of the turn, not permanent, and it does not lower any stats.
