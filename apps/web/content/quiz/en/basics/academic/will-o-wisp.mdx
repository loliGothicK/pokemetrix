question: What is the primary effect of the status move Will-O-Wisp?
options:
  - It inflicts a burn on the target.
  - It deals damage and has a chance to burn the target.
  - It confuses the target.
  - It badly poisons the target.
correctAnswerIndex: 0
reviewed: true
---
【Explanation】
The correct answer is "It inflicts a burn on the target."

Will-O-Wisp is a status move that directly inflicts the Burn status condition on the target. It does not deal direct damage, unlike attacking moves such as Flamethrower or Scald which only have a chance to burn. It also does not confuse the target (like Confuse Ray) or badly poison the target (like Toxic).
