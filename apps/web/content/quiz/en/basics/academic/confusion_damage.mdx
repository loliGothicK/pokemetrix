question: "When a confused Pokémon hurts itself, what type of damage is it calculated as?"
options:
  - "Physical damage"
  - "Special damage"
  - "Fixed damage"
  - "Depends on the category of the move used"
correctAnswer: "Physical damage"
---

Confusion self-damage is always calculated as Physical damage. It is treated as a typeless physical attack with a base power of 40, using the Pokémon's own actual Attack and Defense stats. Even for Special attackers, if their actual Attack stat is low, the damage will be small.
