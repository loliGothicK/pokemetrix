[Explanation]
The Muscle Band boosts the power of the holder's physical moves by 1.1x without any negative side effects.
Boosting physical moves by 1.5x while restricting move choice is the effect of the Choice Band. Boosting special moves by 1.1x is the effect of the Wise Glasses. Boosting Fighting-type moves by 1.2x is the effect of the Black Belt.
