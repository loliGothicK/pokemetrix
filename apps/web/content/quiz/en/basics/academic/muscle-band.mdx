  - Boosts the power of physical moves by 1.1x.
  - Boosts the power of moves by 1.3x, but the holder takes recoil damage.
  - Boosts the power of special moves by 1.1x.
  - Boosts the power of Fighting-type moves by 1.2x.

[Explanation]
The Muscle Band boosts the power of the holder's physical moves by 1.1x without any negative side effects.
Boosting moves by 1.3x with recoil damage is the effect of the Life Orb. Boosting special moves by 1.1x is the effect of the Wise Glasses. Boosting Fighting-type moves by 1.2x is the effect of the Black Belt.
