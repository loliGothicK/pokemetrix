question: "Can a Pokémon holding a Mega Stone simultaneously hold another item like a Focus Sash?"
options:
  - "Yes"
  - "No"
correctAnswer: "No"
---

A Pokémon can only hold one item. If it is holding a Mega Stone, it cannot hold anything else.
