Tailwind doubles the speed of your team for 4 turns, but it takes effect _after_ it is cast. By using Protect on Kangaskhan on the turn Tailwind is set, Kangaskhan avoids taking damage from the faster opponents. On the next turn, Kangaskhan will be faster than both opponents.
