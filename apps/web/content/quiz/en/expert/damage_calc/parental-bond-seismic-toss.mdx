【Explanation】

When a Pokémon with the Parental Bond ability uses a damaging move, the second strike typically deals 25% (0.25x) of the first strike's damage.

However, for moves that deal fixed damage, such as Seismic Toss or Night Shade, this damage reduction modifier is not applied.
Therefore, at Level 50, Seismic Toss will deal exactly 50 damage on the first hit and another 50 damage on the second hit, resulting in a total of 100 damage.
