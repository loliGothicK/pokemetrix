question: "If a Pokémon with the \"Mold Breaker\" Ability uses \"Earthquake\", does it hit an opponent with the \"Levitate\" Ability?"
options:
  - "It hits"
  - "It does not hit"
  - "It hits with resisted damage"
  - "It never hits Pokémon with Levitate under any circumstances"
correctAnswer: "It hits"
---

Mold Breaker (as well as Turboblaze and Teravolt) has the effect of "attacking while ignoring the opponent's Ability." Since Levitate's immunity to Ground-types is ignored, a Mold Breaker Earthquake will hit a Pokémon with Levitate.
