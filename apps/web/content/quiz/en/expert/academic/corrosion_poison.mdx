question: "When a Pokémon with the \"Corrosion\" Ability uses \"Toxic\", what happens to Steel and Poison-type Pokémon?"
options:
  - "It can badly poison both Steel and Poison-types"
  - "It works on Poison-types, but not Steel-types"
  - "It works on Steel-types, but not Poison-types"
  - "It does not work on either"
correctAnswer: "It can badly poison both Steel and Poison-types"

The Corrosion Ability (e.g., Salazzle) allows the user to inflict the poisoned or badly poisoned status condition on Steel and Poison-type Pokémon, which are normally immune. This makes it possible to use Toxic on them.
