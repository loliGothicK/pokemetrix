format: multi_select
question: >-
  When a Pokémon with the Ability 'Sheer Force' holds a 'Life Orb' and uses a
  move with a secondary effect, select all the effects that are nullified (do
  not activate).
options:
  - "The move's secondary effect (e.g., flinch, stat drops)"
  - The recoil damage from 'Life Orb'
  - The damage boost from 'Life Orb' (1.3x)
  - Damage from the target's 'Rough Skin' Ability
correctAnswerIndices:
  - 0
  - 1
reviewed: true
---

【Explanation】
When Sheer Force activates, it nullifies the move's secondary effect and also prevents the recoil damage from Life Orb. However, Life Orb's 1.3x damage boost is NOT nullified, making this a very powerful combination. Note that contact abilities like Rough Skin or the move's own recoil (e.g., Flare Blitz) are still not nullified.
