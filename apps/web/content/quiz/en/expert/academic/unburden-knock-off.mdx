  What happens to the Speed of a Pokémon with 'Unburden' if its held item is

【Explanation】
Unburden triggers and doubles the Pokémon's Speed the moment it loses its held item. The method of losing the item does not matter, whether it's through consumption or by an opponent's moves like Knock Off or Thief.
