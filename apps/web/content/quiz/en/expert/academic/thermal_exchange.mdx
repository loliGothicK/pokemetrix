Thermal Exchange (an Ability possessed by Terapagos, etc.) prevents burns and raises Attack by 1 stage when hit by a Fire-type move. Damage from Fire-type moves is taken normally. This Ability is different from Flash Fire (which nullifies Fire-type moves).
