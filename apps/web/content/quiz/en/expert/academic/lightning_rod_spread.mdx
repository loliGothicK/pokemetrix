  - "Both single-target and area moves are drawn to Lightning Rod."
  - "Neither single-target nor area moves are affected."
  - "Only the area move is drawn to Lightning Rod."
correctAnswer: "The single-target move is drawn to Lightning Rod. The area move is not drawn and hits all targets."

Lightning Rod draws in single-target Electric-type moves and raises Sp. Atk by 1 stage. Multiple-target moves like Discharge are not drawn in and will hit all targets as usual. This mechanic works similarly for "Storm Drain".
