【Explanation】
The highly specific order of operations at the start of a turn is:

1. **Switching Pokémon**: Always happens first, before any moves are executed or Mega Evolutions occur.
2. **Switch-in ability activation**: Abilities that trigger upon entering the battlefield (like Intimidate or Drizzle) resolve immediately after the switch.
3. **Mega Evolution**: Resolves after all switches and their corresponding abilities have completely finished processing.
4. **Quick Claw activation message**: Only after all Mega Evolutions are completed do items like Quick Claw announce their activation, right before the move execution phase begins.

_Note: While a basic turn is just "Switching -> Moves", Expert play requires knowing exactly where specific interruption mechanics and charging messages fit into this timeline._
