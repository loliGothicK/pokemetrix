correctAnswer: "The highest actual stat is multiplied by 1.3 (1.5 for Speed)"

Protosynthesis (an Ability possessed by Paradox Pokémon) boosts the Pokémon's highest actual stat. Stats other than Speed are multiplied by 1.3, while Speed is multiplied by 1.5. If the Pokémon is holding a Booster Energy, the Ability activates even without harsh sunlight, and the item is consumed.
