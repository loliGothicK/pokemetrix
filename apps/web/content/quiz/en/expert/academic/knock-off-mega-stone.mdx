  What happens when a Pokémon holding its corresponding 'Mega Stone' is hit by
  'Knock Off'?

【Explanation】
Corresponding Mega Stones and Z-Crystals cannot be removed by Knock Off. Because the item cannot be knocked off, the move's 1.5x damage multiplier does not activate.
