While Neutralizing Gas is on the battlefield, the Abilities of all other Pokémon (both allies and opponents) are nullified. When the Pokémon with Neutralizing Gas switches out or faints, everyone's Abilities are restored. "Entry hazard Abilities" like Intimidate also will not activate while Neutralizing Gas is on the battlefield.
