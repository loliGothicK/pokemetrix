  When attacking a Pokémon with the Ability 'Unaware' using 'Stored Power' while
  having +6 total stat boosts, what happens?

【Explanation】
Unaware ignores the user's Sp. Atk stage multipliers during damage calculation. However, it does NOT ignore the base power increase of Stored Power, meaning the move hits with 140 BP using the user's raw Sp. Atk stat.
