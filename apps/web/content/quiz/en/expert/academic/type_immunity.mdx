question: "Which of these move types is a Ghost-type Pokémon completely immune to?"
options:
  - "Normal and Fighting"
  - "Poison and Bug"
  - "Normal and Psychic"
  - "Fighting and Ground"
correctAnswer: "Normal and Fighting"
---

Ghost-type Pokémon have two complete immunities: Normal-type and Fighting-type moves. While they resist Bug and Poison, and Dark-types are immune to Psychic, only Normal and Fighting moves are completely negated by the Ghost typing.
