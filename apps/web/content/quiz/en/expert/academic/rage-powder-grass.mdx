  Select all the conditions under which a Pokémon can ignore 'Rage Powder' and
  attack its original target.
options:

  - Pokémon with the Ability 'Overcoat'
  - Pokémon with the Ability 'Unaware'
  - Pokémon with the Ability 'Good as Gold'

  - 0

【Explanation】
Because 'Rage Powder' is a powder move, Grass-types and Pokémon with the Overcoat Ability are immune to its redirection effect (unlike 'Follow Me'). Note that Good as Gold does not ignore Rage Powder, because Rage Powder targets its user, not the opponent.
