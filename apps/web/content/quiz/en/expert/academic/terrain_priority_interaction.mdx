correctAnswer: "Moves with a priority of +1 or higher targeted at grounded Pokémon"

Psychic Terrain nullifies moves with a priority of +1 or higher targeted at grounded Pokémon. Priority moves targeted at airborne Pokémon (such as Flying-types or those with the Levitate Ability) are not nullified. Additionally, moves with a priority of ±0 are not affected.
