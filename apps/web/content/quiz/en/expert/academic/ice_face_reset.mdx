question: 'After a Pokémon with the "Ice Face" Ability (Eiscue) changes to its Noice Face form, what is the condition for it to revert to its original Ice Face form?'
options:
  - 'When the weather becomes "Snow" (or Hail)'
  - "When its HP is fully restored"
  - "When it is switched out to the bench"
  - "It automatically reverts just by the passing of turns"
correctAnswer: 'When the weather becomes "Snow" (or Hail)'
---

The Ice Face Ability negates physical attacks once, but after doing so, the Pokémon changes to its Noice Face form. It reverts to its Ice Face form when the weather becomes "Snow" or "Hail". A characteristic of this Ability is that it does not automatically revert even if the Pokémon is switched out to the bench.
