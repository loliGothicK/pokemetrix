question: "During a turn, your Charizard Mega Evolves into Mega Charizard Y and your opponent switches their Pokémon. Which action happens first?"
options:
  - "The opponent's switch happens first."
  - "Charizard Mega Evolves first."
  - "The order is determined by the Speed stats of the active Pokémon."
  - "The order is determined by the move priority of the incoming Pokémon."
correctAnswer: "The opponent's switch happens first."

Switching out is one of the highest priority actions in a turn and occurs before Mega Evolution, regardless of Speed or move priority. Mega Evolution happens immediately before the execution of standard moves in the turn order.
