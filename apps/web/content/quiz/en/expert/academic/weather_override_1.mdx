question: "Politoed (Drizzle) and Tyranitar (Sand Stream) are sent out at the exact same time. Politoed has higher Speed. Which weather will remain active?"
options:
  - "Rain"
  - "Sandstorm"
  - "They cancel out"
  - "It's random"
correctAnswer: "Sandstorm"
---

Abilities that trigger upon entering the field (like weather setters and Intimidate) activate in Speed order. The faster Pokémon (Politoed) sets Rain first, then the slower Pokémon (Tyranitar) sets Sandstorm, overriding the Rain.
