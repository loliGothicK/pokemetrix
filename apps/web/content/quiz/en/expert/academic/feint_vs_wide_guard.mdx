  - "Only Feint passes through, but the Wide Guard effect remains"
  - "Wide Guard is removed, and it also becomes ineffective on the next turn"
  - "Feint cannot break Wide Guard"
correctAnswer: "The Wide Guard effect for that turn is removed (other area moves will also pass through)"

Feint can bypass and nullify protection moves like Protect and Wide Guard to deal damage. Furthermore, if Feint breaks a protection move, all protection effects for that turn are removed. As a result, subsequent area-of-effect moves and the like will also pass through.
