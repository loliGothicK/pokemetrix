question: "Dark-type Pokémon are immune to status moves boosted by the Prankster ability. Which of the following Prankster-boosted moves will STILL successfully affect a Dark-type opponent?"
options:
  - "Haze"
  - "Taunt"
  - "Thunder Wave"
  - "Parting Shot"
correctAnswer: "Haze"
---

Dark-type Pokémon are immune to Prankster-boosted moves that target them directly. However, moves that target the entire field (like Haze, Sunny Day, or Tailwind) do not check for this immunity, so their effects will still apply to the Dark-type Pokémon.
