question: "Which type is immune to status moves that have their priority increased by Prankster?"
options:
  - "Dark-type only"
  - "Ghost-type only"
  - "Dark and Ghost-type"
  - "Normal-type only"
correctAnswer: "Dark-type only"
---

Status moves that have their priority increased by +1 due to Prankster are nullified against Dark-type Pokémon. The Ghost type is unaffected. Grimmsnarl is a representative Pokémon with Prankster.
