question: "When a Pokémon with the Guts Ability holding a Toxic Orb gets badly poisoned, what is the Attack multiplier from Guts?"
options:
  - "1.5x (the same for both bad poison and regular poison)"
  - "0.5x (due to the large penalty of bad poison)"
  - "2.0x (because bad poison is more severe than regular poison)"
  - "1.3x"
correctAnswer: "1.5x (the same for both bad poison and regular poison)"
---

Guts multiplies Attack by 1.5x when affected by a status condition (poison, bad poison, paralysis, burn, or sleep). The multiplier is the same 1.5x for both bad poison and regular poison. Since Guts ignores the Attack drop from being burned, a burn effectively results in a 1.5x Attack boost (rather than 0.5 * 1.5 = 0.75x, it behaves as no burn reduction + 1.5x boost).
