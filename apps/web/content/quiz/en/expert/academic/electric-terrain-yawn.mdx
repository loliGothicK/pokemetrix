  If a grounded Pokémon is hit by 'Yawn', but sets up 'Electric Terrain' on the
  same turn, what happens?
options:
  - It will not fall asleep at the end of the next turn.
  - It will fall asleep at the end of the next turn.
  - The drowsiness is immediately cured.
  - The Electric Terrain fails to set up.
correctAnswerIndex: 0

【Explanation】
Electric Terrain prevents grounded Pokémon from falling asleep. While it doesn't remove the 'drowsy' status applied by Yawn, it completely blocks the sleep transition at the end of the next turn.
