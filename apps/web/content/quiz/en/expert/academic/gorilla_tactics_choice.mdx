question: "When a Pokémon with the \"Gorilla Tactics\" Ability holds a \"Choice Band\", what happens to its Attack multiplier?"
options:
  - "Only 1.5x (does not stack)"
  - "1.5x × 1.5x = 2.25x"
  - "Becomes 2.0x"
  - "Only the Choice Band effect is applied (1.5x)"
correctAnswer: "Only 1.5x (does not stack)"

Gorilla Tactics increases Attack by 1.5x, similar to Choice Band, but when held at the same time, they do not stack and the multiplier remains at 1.5x. However, since the move-locking effect of Gorilla Tactics also activates while holding a Choice Band, a double lock effect is applied.
