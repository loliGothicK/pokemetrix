Abilities that nullify or reverse Intimidate: **Clear Body** and **White Smoke** (prevent stat drops), **Defiant** and **Competitive** (raise stats when lowered), **Inner Focus** (blocks Intimidate), **Hyper Cutter** (prevents Attack drop), as well as **Oblivious**, **Own Tempo**, and **Scrappy** (all block Intimidate). **Mold Breaker** ignores abilities when attacking, but it does NOT protect against Intimidate.
