Using Trick Room while Trick Room is already active will immediately cancel the effect, returning the battle to standard speed order. While moves like Imprison or Taunt are excellent for preventing Trick Room from being set in the first place, they do nothing to remove it once the space is already twisted. Using Trick Room again is the only direct way to reverse it.
