In modern formats (Generation 8 onwards), dynamic speed mechanics mean that speed changes take effect immediately mid-turn. When Talonflame sets priority Tailwind, Kangaskhan's speed is instantly doubled. This instantly updates the turn order, allowing Kangaskhan to act before Gengar and Garchomp. (In Generation 7 and older, move order was locked at the start of the turn, forcing slower Pokémon to often use Protect while Tailwind was being set).
