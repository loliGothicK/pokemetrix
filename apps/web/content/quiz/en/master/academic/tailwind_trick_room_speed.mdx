question: "If Trick Room is activated while Tailwind (doubles Speed) is already in effect, how is turn order determined?"
options:

  - "Tailwind and Trick Room cancel out, returning Speed to normal."
  - "Trick Room fails if Tailwind is active."
  - "The slowest Pokémon acts first, and the Tailwind effect is immediately removed."
correctAnswer: "Trick Room reverses turn order based on the doubled Speed from Tailwind."
---

Tailwind and Trick Room are completely independent field effects. Tailwind simply doubles the Speed stat of your Pokémon. If Trick Room is then applied, the game reverses the turn order based on these final calculated Speed values. This means the Pokémon benefiting from Tailwind will become incredibly "fast," and thus will act last under Trick Room.
