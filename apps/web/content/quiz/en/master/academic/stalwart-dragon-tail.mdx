【Explanation】
The target redirection immunity of the "Stalwart" Ability activates against the specific **"Pokémon (individual)"** that was selected, rather than the "Location (slot)".

Normally, when a targeted Pokémon leaves the field via switching, Stalwart's individual tracking effect is reset, and the move reverts to attacking the selected slot.
However, if the original target is dragged back onto the field during the exact same turn via a forced switch move like Dragon Tail or Roar, **the target is re-recognized by the game state and the individual tracking effect is restored**.

In this case, even though Incineroar was dragged into the right slot instead of the original left slot due to Ally Switch, Stalwart's tracking applies to the specific individual Pokemon, so Archaludon successfully tracks and hits Incineroar on the right slot.
