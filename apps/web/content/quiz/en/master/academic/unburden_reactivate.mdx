question: "After a Pokémon with the Unburden Ability consumes its item and gains a 2x Speed boost, if it receives another item via a move like Trick and then loses it again, does Unburden reactivate?"
options:
  - "It does not reactivate (Unburden only activates once per battle)"
  - "It reactivates (it activates every time an item is lost)"
  - "It depends on the type of item"
  - "It reactivates, but the Speed boost becomes 1.5x"
correctAnswer: "It does not reactivate (Unburden only activates once per battle)"
---

Unburden only activates once per battle. After it activates from losing an item, if another item is obtained and lost again, it will not reactivate. Additionally, holding an item removes the 2x Speed boost.
