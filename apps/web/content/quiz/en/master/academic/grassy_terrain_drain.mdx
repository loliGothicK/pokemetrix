Grassy Terrain recovers 1/16 of the max HP of grounded Pokémon each turn. It does not provide any additional effect to the recovery amount of draining moves like Draining Kiss or Absorb. It also has the effect of halving the damage of Earthquake, Bulldoze, and Magnitude.
