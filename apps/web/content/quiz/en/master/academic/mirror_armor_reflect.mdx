  - "Intimidate is nullified and there are no stat changes"
  - "Your Attack instead increases by 1 stage"
  - "Intimidate is reflected, and the Attack of all Pokémon on the field drops by 1 stage"
correctAnswer: "The Attack decrease is reflected, and the Attack of the Pokémon that used Intimidate drops by 1 stage"
---

Mirror Armor (an Ability held by Corviknight) reflects stat-lowering effects directed at it back to the source. When affected by Intimidate, the Attack decrease bounces back to the user of Intimidate, lowering the Intimidate user's Attack by 1 stage.
