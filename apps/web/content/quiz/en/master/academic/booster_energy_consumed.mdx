  - "It is consumed (it is always consumed when activated even by weather/terrain)"
  - "It is consumed when the weather ends"
  - "The Booster Energy is consumed and the Ability stays active even without the weather"
correctAnswer: "It is not consumed (activation via weather/terrain does not consume the item)"
---

When the Ability is activated by Harsh Sunlight (Protosynthesis) or Electric Terrain (Quark Drive), the Booster Energy is not consumed. The Booster Energy is only consumed when the Ability is activated in a situation without the weather/terrain.
