question: "If a Pokémon is switched out and sent back in later, can it use Fake Out again on the turn it re-enters the field?"
options:
  - "Yes"
  - "No"
  - "Only if it has Prankster"
  - "Only if the opponent hasn't moved"
correctAnswer: "Yes"
---

Fake Out only requires it to be the first turn the Pokémon is on the field. Switching resets this.
