【Explanation】
Generally, Physical moves make contact and Special moves do not, but there are many exceptions.
"Grass Knot" and "Draining Kiss" are Special moves, but they are classified as contact moves because they involve directly touching the target, making them susceptible to Abilities like Rough Skin.
On the other hand, "Psycho Cut" and "Poltergeist" are Physical moves, but they do not make contact because they attack by launching an aura or manipulating the target's held item from a distance.
