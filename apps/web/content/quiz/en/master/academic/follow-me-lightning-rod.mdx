  The opponent has a Pokémon that used 'Follow Me' and a Pokémon with the
  'Lightning Rod' Ability. You use a single-target Electric-type move with a
  Pokémon that has 'Mold Breaker'. Which is the correct outcome?
options:

  - >-
    The move is redirected to the 'Lightning Rod' Pokémon, and its Sp. Atk
    rises.
  - >-
    The move is redirected to the 'Lightning Rod' Pokémon, but it deals damage
    instead of being nullified.
  - >-
    The move ignores all redirection effects and hits the original intended
    target.

【Explanation】
The Ability 'Mold Breaker' ignores target redirection caused by the opponent's Abilities (like 'Lightning Rod'), but it **cannot ignore redirection effects caused by moves** like 'Follow Me'.
Furthermore, since target redirection from moves ('Follow Me') has a higher priority than redirection from Abilities ('Lightning Rod'), the move will ultimately be drawn to the Pokémon that used 'Follow Me', regardless of whether the attacker has 'Mold Breaker' or not.
