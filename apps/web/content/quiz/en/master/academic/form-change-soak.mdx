question: >-
  If a Pokémon's typing has been changed during battle (for example, into a
  Water-type by the move 'Soak'), and it then undergoes an in-battle form change
  via an ability such as 'Stance Change' or 'Disguise', what happens to its
  typing?
options:
  - >-
    The type-changing effect is removed, and its typing resets to the natural
    types of its new form.
  - "The type-changing effect persists, and it remains a Water-type."
  - "The form change fails, and it remains in its current form as a Water-type."
  - >-
    Pokémon with form-changing abilities are completely immune to moves like
    'Soak' in the first place.
correctAnswerIndex: 0
reviewed: true

**Explanation:**
When a Pokémon undergoes a form change during battle, its base stats and typing are **recalculated and reset to the default data for that new form**.

Therefore, even if a Pokémon's typing was overwritten by a move like 'Soak' or 'Magic Powder', the moment an Aegislash changes to Blade Forme, or a Mimikyu's Disguise is busted, the type-changing effect is completely removed. The Pokémon will immediately revert to its natural typing (e.g., Steel/Ghost or Ghost/Fairy).
