question: >-
  In a Double Battle, your Pokémon A successfully uses 'Protect'. Later in the
  same turn, your allied Pokémon B uses 'Instruct' on A. Which is the correct
  outcome?
options:
  - >-
    It is treated as a consecutive use, so the success rate of 'Protect' becomes
    1/3.
  - "Since it is activated by another move, 'Protect' is guaranteed to succeed."
  - >-
    Since 'Protect' cannot be used consecutively in this way, it is guaranteed
    to fail.
  - "'Instruct' cannot target status moves, so 'Instruct' itself fails."
correctAnswerIndex: 0

【Explanation】
Even when 'Protect' is triggered again in the same turn via 'Instruct', it counts towards the consecutive use counter and undergoes a normal success check. Because Pokémon A already successfully used 'Protect' earlier in the turn, the second 'Protect' triggered by 'Instruct' will have its success rate reduced to 1/3 (approx. 33%).
