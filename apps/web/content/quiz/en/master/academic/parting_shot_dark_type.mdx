Parting Shot is classified as a status move. If its priority is increased by +1 due to Prankster, it becomes ineffective against Dark-type Pokémon. Incineroar does not have Prankster, so this usually doesn't apply, but it becomes relevant in cases where Abilities are swapped using Skill Swap.
