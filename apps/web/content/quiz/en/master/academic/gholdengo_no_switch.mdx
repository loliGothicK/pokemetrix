options:

  - "It nullifies it (prevents all switching to reserve Pokémon)"
  - "Baton Pass fails, but the transfer of stat rank changes occurs"
  - "Only the Pokémon that used Baton Pass becomes unable to switch"
correctAnswer: "It does not nullify it (Baton Pass succeeds)"

Good as Gold prevents switching using "items" (U-turn, Volt Switch, etc.) or support from items, but Baton Pass functions normally. The transfer of stat rank changes via Baton Pass is also valid.
