  A Pokémon uses Knock Off against an opponent with the Rough Skin ability. The attack deals damage, but the user of Knock Off faints from the Rough Skin contact damage.
  What happens to the opponent's held item?

  - The move itself fails because fainting is determined during damage calculation, so the item is not knocked off.

【Explanation】
The item-removing effect of Knock Off still activates after the faint check, just as it normally does.
However, due to a specification change, the effect processing of a move is no longer interrupted even if the attacking Pokémon faints from contact damage.
Therefore, even if the user faints, the effect continues and the opponent's item is knocked off.
