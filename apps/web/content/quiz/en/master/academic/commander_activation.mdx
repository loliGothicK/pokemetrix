The Commander Ability activates when Tatsugiri is on the same field as Dondozo (or its pre-evolution). Tatsugiri goes inside Dondozo and becomes unable to act, but each of Dondozo's stats (Attack, Defense, Special Attack, Special Defense, Speed) increases by 2 stages.
