  Correctly classify the following situations based on whether the move 'Encore'
  will fail or succeed (not fail).
options:
  - The target's last PP-consuming move was 'Encore'.
  - The target hasn't taken any PP-consuming action since entering the field.
  - The target's last PP-consuming move currently has 0 PP left.
  - >-
    The target previously consumed PP, but just failed a move without consuming
    PP.
correctGroups:
  Will Fail:
    - The target's last PP-consuming move was 'Encore'.
    - The target hasn't taken any PP-consuming action since entering the field.
    - The target's last PP-consuming move currently has 0 PP left.
  Will Not Fail:
    - >-
      The target previously consumed PP, but just failed a move without
      consuming PP.
reviewed: true

**Explanation:**
Encore forces the target to repeat its last used move, but it will fail in several specific scenarios:

- **If the target hasn't acted yet** (i.e., hasn't consumed PP on a move since entering the field).
- **If the last move used cannot be Encored** (e.g., Encore itself, Struggle, etc.).
- **If the target has 0 PP left** for the move that would be Encored.

However, if a Pokémon fails to execute a move due to full paralysis, flinching, sleep, or other reasons that prevent PP consumption, the game does not overwrite the "last move used" slot. If the target has consumed PP for a move at least once since entering the field, **Encore will lock the target into that previously used move**. Therefore, Encore will not fail and will succeed in locking the older move.
