  In a Double Battle, the opponent has Rotom (Left) and Garchomp (Right). Your Archaludon, with the Stalwart Ability, selects Flash Cannon targeting the right slot (Garchomp). During that turn, the following events occur:

【Explanation】
The target redirection immunity of the "Stalwart" Ability **activates against the specific "Pokémon (individual)" that was selected, rather than the "Location (slot)".**

In this scenario, the turn resolves as follows:

1. The Switch (highest priority) occurs first. Garchomp leaves, and Tyranitar enters the right slot.
2. Ally Switch occurs next, swapping the positions of Tyranitar and Rotom.

Because the initially targeted Garchomp leaves the field via switching, Stalwart's individual tracking effect is reset.
Having lost its specific target, the attack reverts to standard targeting behavior (attacking the selected "Location"). Therefore, it attacks the right slot, hitting Rotom who moved there via Ally Switch.
