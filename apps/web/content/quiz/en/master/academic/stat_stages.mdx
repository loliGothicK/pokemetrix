question: "If a Pokémon maximizes its Attack stat to +6 stages (e.g., using Belly Drum), what is the multiplier applied to its physical damage?"
options:
  - "4.0x (400%)"
  - "3.0x (300%)"
  - "6.0x (600%)"
  - "2.5x (250%)"
correctAnswer: "4.0x (400%)"
---

Stat boosts scale incrementally. At +6 stages, a stat is multiplied by (2 + 6) / 2 = 8 / 2 = 4.0x.
