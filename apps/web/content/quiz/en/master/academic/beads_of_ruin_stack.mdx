question: "If multiple Pokémon activate 'Ruin' Abilities such as Vessel of Ruin, Tablets of Ruin, Sword of Ruin, and Beads of Ruin at the same time, do their effects stack?"
options:
  - "They do not stack (only one instance of the effect is applied)"
  - "They stack (if there are two, the opponent's Defense becomes 1/2 * 1/2 = 1/4)"
  - "It depends on which one entered the battlefield first"
  - "The same Ability does not stack, but different Abilities (like Vessel and Tablets) stack"
correctAnswer: "They do not stack (only one instance of the effect is applied)"
---

Ruin Abilities (possessed by Iron Crown, etc.) do not stack even if multiple are on the battlefield at the same time, and only one instance is applied. For example, even if there are two Pokémon with Vessel of Ruin, the opponent's Special Defense remains at 0.75x.
