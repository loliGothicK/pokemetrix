Priority levels: Helping Hand (+5) > Fake Out (+3) > Earthquake (±0) > Trick Room (-7). Helping Hand boasts a massive +5 priority, allowing it to easily outspeed Fake Out. Trick Room has an extremely low priority of -7, meaning it goes last; however, there is virtually no situation where you'd want to use it before an opponent's attack anyway.
