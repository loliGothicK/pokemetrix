  Order the following factors in the sequence they are applied when calculating
  a Pokémon's final Speed stat.

【Explanation】
In Speed calculations, stat stage multipliers are applied first. Then, multipliers from field conditions like Tailwind, followed by Items and Abilities, are applied sequentially. Finally, the Paralysis modifier is applied.
