  While 'Grassy Terrain' is active, you attack the opponent with 'Earthquake'.
  Select all situations where the 'damage halving effect' of Grassy Terrain **is
  applied to the damage calculation**.
options:

  - When a Pokémon with 'Mold Breaker' attacks a Pokémon with 'Levitate'
  - When attacking a Flying-type Pokémon holding an 'Iron Ball'
  - >-
    When attacking a Pokémon with 'Levitate' that is under the effect of 'Smack

【Explanation】
A Pokémon must be 'grounded' to receive the damage halving effect of Grassy Terrain, but there are exceptions and traps in the mechanics.
- **Dig**: Hitting a target underground with Earthquake doubles the damage. However, there is a specific rule that Pokémon with the **'Underground' status (or 'Airborne' status, etc.) completely ignore all field damage modifiers**. Therefore, the Grassy Terrain's 0.5x reduction is bypassed, and they take the full 2.0x damage (not applied).
- **Mold Breaker vs Levitate**: 'Mold Breaker' ignores the Ability 'Levitate' to land a hit, but it does not ground the target. Thus, the Terrain effect is not applied, resulting in full damage (not applied).
- **Iron Ball / Smack Down**: Both of these effects force an ungrounded Pokémon to become 'grounded', making them susceptible to Terrain effects like the Earthquake damage reduction (applied).
