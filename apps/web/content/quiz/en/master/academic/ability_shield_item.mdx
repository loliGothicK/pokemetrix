question: "The Ability of a Pokémon holding an Ability Shield cannot be changed by the opponent's moves or Abilities, but does Skill Swap work?"
options:
  - "It works (Skill Swap cannot be blocked by Ability Shield)"
  - "It does not work (Ability Shield blocks all Ability changes)"
  - "The user's Ability becomes the Ability of the Ability Shield holder, but the holder's Ability does not change"
  - "A Pokémon holding an Ability Shield cannot be targeted by Skill Swap"
correctAnswer: "It works (Skill Swap cannot be blocked by Ability Shield)"
---

Ability Shield is an item that "prevents effects that change the holder's Ability caused by the opponent," but since Skill Swap is a move that "exchanges" Abilities with each other, rather than a defensive Ability change, it works. You can steal the Ability of a Pokémon holding an Ability Shield with Skill Swap.
