  - "Only the opponent's Defense increases are ignored (your Attack decreases are included in the calculation)"

  - "All stat rank changes are ignored when a critical hit lands"
correctAnswer: "Both the opponent's beneficial stat rank increases and your detrimental stat rank decreases are ignored"

When a critical hit lands, "your detrimental stat rank changes (such as Attack drops)" and "the opponent's beneficial stat rank changes (such as Defense boosts)" are ignored. However, your beneficial stat rank changes (Attack boosts) and the opponent's detrimental stat rank changes (Defense drops) are included in the calculation normally.
