Zero to Hero activates when Palafin (Zero Form) is returned to the party (via switching or moves like Flip Turn), causing it to transform. Once it assumes its Hero Form, it will not revert to its Zero Form during the battle. In Double Battles, it requires an intentional sequence to switch it back into the party.
