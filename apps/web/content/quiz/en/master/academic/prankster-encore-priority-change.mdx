question: >-
  A Pokémon with the Prankster ability uses Encore on an opponent. The opponent used a priority ±0 move on the previous turn.
  This turn, the opponent selected a priority +1 move. However, Encore (which has +1 priority due to Prankster) goes first because of a higher Speed stat, and the opponent becomes Encored into their priority ±0 move from the previous turn.
  What happens to the opponent's action this turn?
options:
  - The Encored priority ±0 move is executed in the +1 priority bracket.
  - The turn order is recalculated mid-turn, and the Encored move is executed in the ±0 priority bracket.
  - The move fails because the priority of the Encored move does not match the priority of the move selected at the start of the turn.
  - The opponent becomes Encored, but still executes the priority +1 move they originally selected for this turn.

【Explanation】
In *Pokémon Champions*, the mechanics have been adjusted so that turn order is dynamically recalculated mid-turn based on the true priority of the move forced by Encore.
Therefore, if a Pokémon that selected a priority +1 move becomes Encored into a priority ±0 move, its turn order is recalculated to match priority ±0, and the move will execute successfully in that turn order.
