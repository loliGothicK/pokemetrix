  - "Clear Body"
  - "Gooey"
  - "White Smoke"
correctGroups:
  Debuff:
    - "Intimidate"
    - "Gooey"
  Defense:
    - "Clear Body"
    - "White Smoke"
---

Intimidate (lowers opponent's Attack) and Gooey (lowers the Speed of an attacker that makes contact) are debuff abilities that weaken the opponent. Clear Body and White Smoke are defensive abilities that prevent your own stats from being lowered, serving as excellent counters to Intimidate.
