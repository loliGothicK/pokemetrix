question: "When two layers of Toxic Spikes are on the opponent's side of the field, what status condition is inflicted on grounded Pokémon that switch in?"
options:
  - "Badly poisoned"
  - "Regularly poisoned"
  - "Paralyzed"
  - "Nothing happens"
correctAnswer: "Badly poisoned"
---

One layer of Toxic Spikes inflicts regular poison, while two layers inflict bad poison. When a grounded Poison-type Pokémon switches in, it absorbs and removes all layers of Toxic Spikes. Steel-type and Poison-type Pokémon are immune to being poisoned or badly poisoned by them. Flying-type or levitating Pokémon are unaffected.
