  If Psychic Terrain is active, and your Pokémon with the Mold Breaker ability
  uses a priority move against an opponent's Pokémon with the Levitate ability,
  will the opponent be protected?
options:
  - Protected.
  - Not protected.
correctAnswerIndex: 0
reviewed: true
---

【Explanation】
Mold Breaker ignores the target's ability during the execution of a move.
Because of this, the opponent's Levitate is ignored, and they are treated as grounded.
As a result, the effect of Psychic Terrain (which protects grounded Pokémon from priority moves) applies, and the opponent is protected.
