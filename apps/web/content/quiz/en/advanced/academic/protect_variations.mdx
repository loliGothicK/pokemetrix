  - "They do not share the penalty (each is counted independently)"

  - "Only King's Shield is counted independently"
correctAnswer: "They share the penalty (using one will lower the success rate of other protection moves on the next turn)"

Protecting moves such as Protect, Detect, King's Shield, and Obstruct all share the same success rate counter. After using any of them, the success rate of another protection move drops to about 1/3 on the following turn.
