【Explanation】
Baton Pass is an excellent pivot move that transfers stat changes, Substitute, and etc. However, you must be careful because it also passes negative conditions like Leech Seed (though it does not pass major status conditions like Burn). It can also uniquely bypass trapping effects like Shadow Tag or Mean Look.
