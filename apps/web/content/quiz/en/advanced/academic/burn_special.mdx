question: "Your Gengar gets Burned by Will-O-Wisp. It is a special attacker. How does the Burn status affect its Shadow Ball (Special) damage?"
options:
  - "It has no effect on Special attack damage."
  - "It halves its Special attack damage."
  - "It reduces its Special attack damage by 25%."
  - "It causes Gengar to flinch."
correctAnswer: "It has no effect on Special attack damage."
---

The Burn status condition only halves the damage of PHYSICAL attacks. Special attacks like Shadow Ball deal full damage, meaning special attackers do not care about being burned aside from the chip damage.
