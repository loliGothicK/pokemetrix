【Explanation】
Although Body Press uses Defense for damage calculation, it remains a physical attack and is thus halved by a Burn. Because the damage calculation applies Defense stat stage increases, boosting your Defense with a move like Iron Defense simultaneously increases your physical bulk and your attacking power. This essentially grants the effect of both Iron Defense and Swords Dance in a single turn, making it a highly potent offensive and defensive strategy.
