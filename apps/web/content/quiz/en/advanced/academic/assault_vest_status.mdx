options:

  - "Only status moves that lower stats"
  - "Only self-destruct moves (Self-Destruct, Explosion, etc.)"
correctAnswer: "All status moves"

Holding an Assault Vest increases Sp. Def by 1.5x, but prevents the use of all status moves. Helping Hand, Tailwind, Protect, etc. cannot be used. However, "Transform" is classified as a status move but is exceptionally usable.
