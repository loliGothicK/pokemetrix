question: "What is the condition for receiving the benefits of terrain effects (such as Electric Terrain)?"
options:
  - "Being grounded (not floating)"
  - "Matching the terrain's type"
  - "Having full HP"
  - "Being an ally of the Pokemon that set the terrain"
correctAnswer: "Being grounded (not floating)"
---

Terrain effects do not apply to "floating" Pokemon, such as those with the Flying type, the Levitate ability, or those affected by Magnet Rise or Fly. Only grounded Pokemon receive the benefits. If a floating Pokemon is brought to the ground by Gravity, the terrain effects will apply to them.
