question: "Which of the following volatile status conditions are cured by the Mental Herb?"
options:
  - "Infatuation, Disable, Encore, Torment, and Taunt"
  - "Infatuation only"
  - "Confusion and Infatuation"
  - "All status conditions (including Burn, Paralysis, etc.)"
correctAnswer: "Infatuation, Disable, Encore, Torment, and Taunt"
---

The Mental Herb is consumed the moment the holder is inflicted with Infatuation, Disable, Encore, Torment, or Taunt, curing the condition. It disappears after one use. It is nullified by Gholdengo's Good as Gold.
