question: "Incineroar has the Ability 'Intimidate'. When it enters a Double Battle, how many opponents does Intimidate affect?"
options:
  - "Affects all 2 opponents on the field"
  - "Affects only 1 opponent (the one with the higher Speed)"
  - "Affects only 1 opponent (randomly chosen)"
  - "Affects only 1 opponent of your choice"
correctAnswer: "Affects all 2 opponents on the field"
---

In Double Battles, Intimidate lowers the Attack of all Pokémon on the opponent's side of the field (up to 2 Pokémon) by 1 stage each when entering the battle. In the case of Incineroar, it lowers the Attack of both opponents on the field by 1 stage.
