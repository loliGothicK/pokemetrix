question: "When hitting a Pokemon holding a Rocky Helmet with a contact move, what fraction of the attacker's maximum HP is taken as damage?"
options:

  - "1/8 of maximum HP"
  - "1/4 of maximum HP"
  - "1/16 of maximum HP"
correctAnswer: "1/6 of maximum HP"
---

The Rocky Helmet inflicts damage equal to 1/6 of the attacker's maximum HP when they use a contact move against the holder. It triggers even on spread moves, as long as they make contact. It does not trigger on non-contact moves like Surf.
