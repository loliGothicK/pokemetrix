question: "Your Gengar and the opponent's Gengar are both on the field. Both have exactly the same Speed stat (Speed Tie). Who moves first?"
options:
  - "It is a 50/50 random coin flip every turn."
  - "The host of the game moves first."
  - "The Gengar with higher HP moves first."
  - "The Gengar that was sent out last moves first."
correctAnswer: "It is a 50/50 random coin flip every turn."

When two Pokémon have the exact same Speed stat and use moves in the same priority bracket, the turn order between them is decided by a random 50% chance. This is known as a Speed Tie.
