correctAnswer: "Protects both the user and allies on the field from multi-target moves"

Wide Guard protects the user and all allies on the field from multi-target moves (such as Earthquake, Heat Wave, etc.) for that turn. It cannot block single-target moves. Like Protect, its success rate decreases if used consecutively.
