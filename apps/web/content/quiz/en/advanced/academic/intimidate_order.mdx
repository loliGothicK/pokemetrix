question: "In a Double Battle, if two Pokémon with Intimidate enter your side of the field at the same time, in what order does Intimidate activate?"
options:
  - "The Intimidate of the Pokémon with the higher Speed activates first"
  - "Activates randomly"
  - "Both Intimidates activate simultaneously"
  - "Activates in the order chosen by the player"
correctAnswer: "The Intimidate of the Pokémon with the higher Speed activates first"
---

When Abilities trigger at the same timing, the Ability of the Pokémon with the higher Speed is prioritized. Similarly, if two Pokémon with Intimidate enter on the same turn, the faster one's Ability will activate first.
