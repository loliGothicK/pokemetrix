Gravity brings "floating" Pokémon—such as Flying types, those with the Levitate Ability, those under the effect of Magnet Rise, or those in the semi-invulnerable turn of Fly—down to the ground for 5 turns. Additionally, the accuracy of all moves on the field is multiplied by 1.67 (5/3). Moves like Fly, Bounce, etc. also become unusable.
