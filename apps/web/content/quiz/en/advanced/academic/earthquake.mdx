【Explanation】
The correct answer is "This move's power is doubled against targets that have the Underground status. When Grassy Terrain is active this move's power is halved.".

Earthquake is a powerful Ground-type physical move. If the target is in the semi-invulnerable turn of Dig, Earthquake will hit them and deal twice the normal damage. It cannot hit Pokémon that are airborne (like those using Fly) or those with the Levitate ability. In double battles, Earthquake hits all adjacent Pokémon on the field, which means it will also damage your own allies, unlike moves such as Earth Power.
