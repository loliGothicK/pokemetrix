question: "Does the doubled Speed effect of Tailwind apply only to the user, or does it also apply to allies?"
options:
  - "It applies to the user and all allies on the field"
  - "It applies only to the user"
  - "It applies only to the slowest ally"
  - "The user's Speed is quadrupled"
correctAnswer: "It applies to the user and all allies on the field"
---

The doubled Speed effect of Tailwind applies not only to the user, but to all allies on your side of the field. Pokemon that are switched in during its effect will also continue to benefit from it.
