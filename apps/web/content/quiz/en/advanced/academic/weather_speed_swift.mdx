question: "By what multiplier is the Speed of a Pokemon with the Chlorophyll (in Sun) or Swift Swim (in Rain) ability increased?"
options:
  - "2x"
  - "1.5x"
  - "3x"
  - "1.3x"
correctAnswer: "2x"
---

Abilities like Chlorophyll (in Sun), Swift Swim (in Rain), Sand Rush (in Sandstorm), and Slush Rush (in Hail/Snow) all double the Pokemon's Speed in their respective weather conditions. If stacked with Tailwind, their Speed effectively becomes quadrupled.
