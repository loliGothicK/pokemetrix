Pokemetrix is a comprehensive toolset designed to support competitive Pokémon battling.
From team building and damage calculation to battle logging and analytics, our goal is to provide a single platform that covers the entire competitive battling lifecycle.

## The Core Workflow

Pokemetrix is designed around a continuous cycle of building, testing, battling, and analyzing:

1. **Build (Team Builder / My BOX)**
   - Create new teams and tweak your Pokémon using the **Team Builder**.
   - Save your fully trained Pokémon to **My BOX** so you can easily reuse them across multiple teams without re-entering their stats.
2. **Test (Damage Calc)**
   - Use the **Damage Calc** to verify how your Pokémon match up against popular meta threats.
   - Adjust EVs, items, or natures in the Team Builder based on your calculations.
3. **Battle & Log (Battle Record)**
   - Play your matches and log the results using **Battle Record**.
   - Track the opponent's team, your leads, match outcomes, and save important notes for later review.
4. **Analyze (Winrate Insights)**
   - Visualize your performance with **Winrate Insights**.
   - Identify weak points, common threats, and win/loss causes to refine your strategy and start the cycle over.

## Key Features

- **[Team Builder](/docs/team-builder)**
  - Build teams with detailed stat, EV, item, and move configurations.
  - Full support for Pokepaste import and export.
- **[My BOX](/docs/my-box)**
  - A personal storage for your ready-to-battle Pokémon to speed up future team building.
- **[Damage Calc](/docs/damage-calc)**
  - Highly accurate calculator for both Single and Double battles, including fields, weather, and stat modifiers.
- **[Battle Record](/docs/battle-record)**
  - Keep track of every match, including team matchups, selected Pokémon, and detailed notes per season.
- **[Winrate Insights](/docs/battle-analytics)**
  - Build your own custom dashboards with various widgets to deeply analyze your win rates and trends.

## Why Create an Account?

You can use Pokemetrix without logging in (data is saved to your browser's local storage). However, creating a free account provides several benefits:

- **Cloud Sync**: Safely store your teams and battle records in the cloud, allowing you to access them seamlessly across your PC and mobile devices.
- **Data Security**: Prevent accidental data loss from browser cache clears.
- **Advanced Features**: Unlock access to advanced tools like custom analytics dashboards.
