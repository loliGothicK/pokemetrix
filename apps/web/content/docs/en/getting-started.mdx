# Getting Started

Pokemetrix is a collection of tools for competitive Pokémon battling:

- **Team Builder** — build and manage your teams
- **Damage Calculator** — calculate damage ranges for any matchup
- **Battle Record** — log battles and track your results
- **Battle Analytics** — analyze your win rates and matchups

Pick a tool from the sidebar to get started.
