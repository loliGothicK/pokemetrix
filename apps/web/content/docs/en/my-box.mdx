## Overview

My BOX is a feature where you can store and manage the Pokémon you use in battles.
It works seamlessly with the Team Builder.

## Box Features

You can register individual Pokémon here with their stats, items, abilities, and moves.
These Pokémon can then be easily dragged into teams in the Team Builder.
