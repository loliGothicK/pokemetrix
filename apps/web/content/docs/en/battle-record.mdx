## Overview

The Battle Record feature helps you log and manage the outcomes of your Pokémon battles on a match-by-match basis.
You can organize your records by seasons, and save detailed information about the opponent's team, your leads, and specific notes.

## Creating a Season

Before you can start recording battles, you need to create a **Season**.

1. Click **"New season"** in the sidebar.
2. Set the season name, format (Single/Double), ruleset, and time period.
3. Click "Save" to finish creating it.

## Recording a Match

With a season selected, click the **"Record battle"** button to open the log form.

### Information You Can Log

| Field           | Description                                                            |
| --------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Outcome         | Win / Loss / Draw                                                      |
| Your Team       | Select the team you used from the Team Builder                         |
| Selection       | Tap your Pokémon to toggle their roles (In the back / Lead / Not used) |
| Opponent's Team | Register the opponent's Pokémon (including abilities, items, moves)    |
| Rating          | Your rating after the match                                            |
| Notes           | Write down key plays, threats, and reads                               |
| Tags            | Categorize matches (e.g., archetype, gimmick)                          |

### Recording Selections

Tap each Pokémon in your team panel to toggle its selection status.
(The status cycles: `Not used` → `In the back` → `Lead` → `Not used` ...)

## Match History

A list of all recorded matches will be displayed for the active season.
Clicking on any record allows you to view its full details and edit it.

## Deleting Records

You can delete a match record by clicking the delete button on its detail page. This action cannot be undone.

## Login and Cloud Sync

If you are logged in, your battle records are securely stored in the cloud.
We highly recommend creating an account if you want to access your logs from multiple devices.
