## Overview

Use the Battle Record feature to keep track of your match results, selected Pokémon, and opponents.
By recording your battles, you can later analyze your performance in the Analytics section.

## Key Features

You can log the **Outcome** (Win/Loss), the Pokémon you brought to the battle, and specific notes about the match.
