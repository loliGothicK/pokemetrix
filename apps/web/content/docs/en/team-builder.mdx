## Overview

The Team Builder allows you to create and organize groups of up to 6 Pokémon for battles.
You can experiment with different compositions, typings, and strategies.

## Getting Started

Click **New** in the Team Builder section to create a new blank team.
You can then add Pokémon manually or pull them from your BOX.
