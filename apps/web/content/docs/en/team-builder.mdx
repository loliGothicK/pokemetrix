## Overview

The Team Builder is your primary tool for constructing and managing Pokémon teams for competitive play.
You can register up to 6 Pokémon per team and configure their items, abilities, moves, and EV spreads in detail.

## Creating a Team

Click the **New** button at the top of the screen (or from the sidebar) to create a brand new team.
You can rename your team at any time by clicking on the team name field.

## Adding Pokémon

Clicking on an empty slot in your team will open the search dialog.

- Search by name (e.g., `Garchomp`)
- Filter by type (e.g., `@type:Dragon`)

Once a Pokémon is selected, it will be added to the slot and its details panel will open.

## Basic Configuration

### Item, Ability, and Nature

For each slot, you can set the Pokémon's **Item**, **Ability**, and **Nature** from the details panel.
If the Pokémon can Mega Evolve, a "Mega" button will be available to change its form.

### Selecting Moves

Click the move slots (up to 4 times) to assign moves to your Pokémon.
The move drawer provides detailed information such as Power, Accuracy, Type, and Priority to help you choose the best options.

## EV Spreads

Switch to the **EV Spreads** tab to configure Effort Values for each stat (up to a maximum of 32 per stat).
The inputs are restricted so you cannot exceed the total limit of 66. The remaining EV count updates in real-time as you drag sliders or enter numbers.

## Import and Export

| Action | Description |
|---|---|
| **Import from Pokepaste URL** | Paste a Pokepaste link to load a team instantly. |
| **Import from Text** | Directly paste Pokepaste-formatted text. |
| **Copy to Clipboard** | Copy the team in Pokepaste format. |
| **Create Pokepaste** | Publish your team to Pokepaste and get a shareable link. |

## Saving Your Team

Your team is automatically saved to the cloud if you are logged in, ensuring you can access it across devices.
If you are using the app as a guest, data is saved locally to your browser's storage.

## Sharing Teams

Click the **Share Team** button on the team details page to generate a shareable link.
You can choose whether to include the exact stats and EV spreads in the shared version.
