## Overview

Pokétistix is an integrated competitive battle toolkit designed to streamline team preparation, in-depth post-match reviews, and data-driven team optimization.

This guide walks you through the practical steps to execute the core competitive cycle:

```text
[1. Build Team] ───> [2. Record Battle] ───> [3. Analyze Data] ───> [4. Iterate & Refine]
  Team Builder          Battle Record Form       Analytics & Dashboard     EV / Move Adjustments
```

---

## Step 1: Manage Teams in Team Builder

Start by registering and configuring your competitive team in the [Team Builder](/docs/team-builder).

### 1. Create a New Team

1. Open **"Teams"** from the navigation menu.
2. Click the **"New"** button at the top of the screen to generate a fresh team.
3. Name your team (e.g., `Champions Season 1`). This name will be selectable when logging battle records later.
4. If you already have a Pokepaste export, you can click **"Import"** to import directly via URL or paste text.

### 2. Add Pokémon and Configure Specs

1. Click any of the 6 slots to open the Pokémon selection dialog.
2. Search by name or filter by type to choose your Pokémon.
3. In the detail drawer, configure the following attributes:
   - **Held Item**
   - **Ability**
   - **Nature**
   - **Moves (up to 4)**: Choose moves with real-time visibility into type, power, accuracy, and priority.
   - **Mega Evolution**: For Pokémon capable of Mega Evolution, use the "Mega Evolve" button to toggle forms.

### 3. Configure EVs and Survival Tuning

- **EV Bounds**:
  - In Pokémon Champions format, stats are capped at **32 EVs per stat** and **66 total EVs** across all 6 stats. The UI enforces these limits in real time.
- **Survival Tuning**:
  - Click the "Survival Tuning" button on the EV tab to open an automatic bulk optimization modal.
  - Specify the attacker's species, move, nature, held item, weather, and screens (Reflect / Light Screen) to calculate and apply the optimal HP, Defense, and Sp. Def spread to guarantee or maximize survival odds.

### 4. Save Your Team

- When authenticated, teams synchronize automatically to the cloud.
- Click the **"Save"** button in the top right to persist your configuration.

## Step 2: Record Battles (Battle Record)

After completing matches on the ranked ladder, record your results and selections in [Battle Record](/docs/battle-record).

### 1. Prepare a Season

Battle records are organized and aggregated by season.

1. Click **"New Season"** in the sidebar or top bar.
2. Enter the season name, format (**Singles** or **Doubles**), regulation, and date range.

### 2. Open the Battle Record Dialog

- With your active season selected, click **"Record Battle"**.
- On desktop, you can also press the **`N`** keyboard hotkey to open the dialog instantly.

### 3. Select Your Team and Member Roles

1. **Choose Your Team**:
   - In the "Your Team" dropdown, select the team you created in Step 1. All 6 team members will load automatically.
2. **Set Member Selection Roles**:
   - Click or tap each Pokémon card to cycle through its battle status:
     - **Unselected** (default border)
     - **Back** (blue border + blue indicator dot)
     - **Lead** (yellow border + yellow indicator dot)
   - For Doubles, the header displays your current lead/back counts against the format limit (2 Leads, 2 Backs).

### 4. Record the Opponent Team and Roles

1. **Add Opponent Species**:
   - Click the empty slot (`+` icon) to quickly search and add up to 6 opponent Pokémon.
2. **Mark Opponent Selection**:
   - Tap the opponent cards to cycle between **Lead (yellow)**, **Back (blue)**, and **Unselected**.
3. **Add Known Match Intel**:
   - Hover over an opponent card (or tap on mobile) and click the pencil icon to open the Opponent Details dialog.
   - Note down revealed held items, abilities, moves, or tactical notes observed during the match.

### 5. Record Result, Rating, Tags, and Notes

- **Result**:
  - Click **"WIN"**, **"LOSS"**, or **"DRAW"**, or press the **`W`**, **`L`**, or **`D`** hotkeys.
- **Post-Match Rating**:
  - Enter your new rating (e.g., `1650`). The rating delta from your previous match (e.g., `+16`) will be computed automatically.
- **Taxonomy Tags**:
  - Tag archetype elements and opponent gimmicks such as `trick-room`, `tailwind`, `weather-rain`, or `intimidate`. Custom tags are also supported.
- **Match Notes**:
  - Document key turning points, misplays, or unexpected sets.
- **Save**:
  - Press **`Ctrl + S`** (or **`Cmd + S`** on macOS) or click **"Save"** to submit the record.

## Step 3: Inspect and Analyze Your Data

As you accumulate battle records, Pokétistix unlocks multiple analytical views to review your performance.

### 1. Review Recent Matches in Battle Record (`/battle-record`)

- View a chronological timeline of your matches with win/loss badges and rating trends.
- Use the **Stats Bar** at the top to check your current rating, latest rating delta, overall win rate, and W/L/D bar breakdown.
- Filter matches using the **"All"**, **"Win"**, **"Loss"**, and **"Draw"** tabs.
- Click any match card to reopen the details, check your selections, and review opponent notes.

### 2. High-Level Meta Analysis in Battle Analytics (`/battle-analytics`)

Click **"Analytics"** in the top navigation to view aggregate statistics for the current season.

- **Overall Stats**: Total matches, win percentage, and total wins/losses.
- **Top Opponents**:
  - A ranked list of the most frequent Pokémon encountered in the opponent's team.
  - Visual progress bars show your specific win rate against each threat, helping you identify metagame trends and matchup vulnerabilities.

### 3. Deep Custom Inspection with Dashboard (`/dashboard`)

On desktop devices, access the customizable **Dashboard** BI tool to construct tailored analytics workspaces.

| Widget Template                         | Key Analytical Insight                                                                |
| --------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Win Rate Summary**                    | High-level overall win percentage at a glance                                         |
| **Lead / Back Performance**             | Compares win rates when specific Pokémon are chosen as Leads vs Backs                 |
| **Lead Matchup Matrix (Pivot Heatmap)** | Visualizes win rates for each combination of your Leads vs opponent Leads             |
| **Win/Loss Cause Breakdown**            | Measures the statistical impact of specific opponent Pokémon or tags on your win rate |
| **Rating History**                      | Line chart tracking rating trajectory and momentum over time                          |
| **Recent Matches & Streaks**            | Shows recent form and active winning/losing streaks                                   |

- **Dynamic Variables**: Use dropdown variable selectors to switch between seasons without modifying your dashboard layout.

## Step 4: Refine and Iterate Your Team

Data analysis transforms raw match history into actionable team improvements:

1. **Patch Weak Matchups**:
   - If the Win/Loss Cause Breakdown or Top Opponents list reveals a struggling matchup, revisit the Team Builder to adjust moves, swap held items, or replace a flex slot.
2. **Update Bulk Spreads**:
   - If match notes indicate that a key Pokémon was consistently knocked out by a specific attack, run the Survival Tuning tool to recalibrate your EV spreads.
3. **Standardize Proven Leads**:
   - Identify your highest win-rate lead pairs from the Pivot Heatmap and document them in your game plan.

By repeating this feedback loop, you build a resilient, battle-tested team backed by hard data.
