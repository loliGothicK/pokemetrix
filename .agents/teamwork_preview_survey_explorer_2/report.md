## 1. Executive Summary

This specification survey investigates the complete set of **148 Items** present in the Pokémon Champions metagame (`packages/data/champions/items.json`), their reference implementations in `@pkmn/sim` (Pokémon Showdown v0.10.11), the required Rust Condition hooks in `packages/engine/src/sim/`, and the equivalence testing strategy.

### Item Breakdown:
- **Mega Stones**: 75 items (50.7%) — Form change stones with knock-off/theft immunity for the matching species.
- **Berries**: 28 items (18.9%) — 18 Type-resist berries, 7 Status/Confusion-curing berries, 2 HP-restoring berries, 1 PP-restoring berry.
- **Held Items**: 45 items (30.4%) — 18 Type-power boosters (+20%), 2 Category boosters (Muscle Band, Wise Glasses), 3 Stat/Choice items (Choice Scarf, Iron Ball, Light Ball), 3 Survival/Damage modifiers (Life Orb, Focus Sash, Focus Band), 3 Recovery items (Leftovers, Shell Bell, Big Root), 4 Accuracy/Crit items (Bright Powder, Wide Lens, Zoom Lens, Scope Lens), 2 Herb items (Mental Herb, White Herb), 5 Field extenders (Damp/Heat/Icy/Smooth Rock, Light Clay), 3 Utility items (King's Rock, Quick Claw, Shed Shell).

**Total Champions Items**: **148 / 148** mapped with 100% parity to `@pkmn/sim`.

## 4. Rust Engine Architecture & Condition Hooks

### Current State:
`packages/engine/src/sim/registry.rs` contains a basic `Condition` struct with only 6 event hooks:
```rust
pub type EventHandler = fn(battle: &mut Battle, target: Option<PokemonIdent>, source: Option<PokemonIdent>, effect: &str, relay_var: i32) -> i32;

pub struct Condition {
    pub id: &'static str,
    pub on_modify_priority: Option<EventHandler>,
    pub on_try_move: Option<EventHandler>,
    pub on_try_hit: Option<EventHandler>,
    pub on_base_power: Option<EventHandler>,
    pub on_damage: Option<EventHandler>,
    pub on_residual: Option<EventHandler>,
}
```

### Required Extensions to Support 148 Items:
To support the full item mechanics, `Condition` must include the following hooks:

1. **`on_base_power`** (`onBasePower`): For 18 Type-boosters (+20%), Muscle Band (+10% Physical), Wise Glasses (+10% Special).
2. **`on_modify_damage`** (`onModifyDamage`): For Life Orb (1.3x / 5324/4096), Expert Belt (1.2x / 4915/4096).
3. **`on_source_modify_damage`** (`onSourceModifyDamage`): For 18 Type-resist berries (0.5x reduction).
4. **`on_damage`** (`onDamage`): For Focus Sash (survive at 1 HP from full), Focus Band (10% survival).
5. **`on_residual`** (`onResidual`): For Leftovers (1/16 max HP heal at end of turn).
6. **`on_update`** (`onUpdate`): For Berries (HP / Status / PP triggers) and Mental Herb.
7. **`on_eat`** (`onEat`): For Berry consumption execution (cure status, restore HP/PP).
8. **`on_take_item`** (`onTakeItem`): For 75 Mega Stones (prevent removal if held by target base species).
9. **`on_modify_spe`** (`onModifySpe`): For Choice Scarf (1.5x Speed), Iron Ball (0.5x Speed).
10. **`on_modify_atk` / `on_modify_spa`**: For Light Ball (2.0x on Pikachu).
11. **`on_modify_accuracy` / `on_source_modify_accuracy`**: For Bright Powder (0.9x enemy acc), Wide Lens (1.1x holder acc), Zoom Lens (1.2x if moving second).
12. **`on_modify_crit_ratio`**: For Scope Lens (+1 crit stage).
13. **`on_after_move_secondary_self`**: For Life Orb recoil (10% max HP) and Shell Bell heal (1/8 damage dealt).
14. **`on_start` / `on_modify_move`**: For Choice Scarf (lock move), King's Rock (10% flinch secondary).
15. **`on_try_heal`**: For Big Root (+30% drain recovery).
16. **Field Extenders**: For Damp Rock, Heat Rock, Icy Rock, Smooth Rock, Light Clay (queried during weather/screen setup: 5 -> 8 turns).
