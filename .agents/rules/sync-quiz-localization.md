---
name: sync-quiz-localization
description: Always update all language variants when modifying quiz content
trigger: always_on
---
When modifying, correcting, or updating any quiz content (such as questions, options, correct answers, or explanations), you MUST ensure that all corresponding language variants (e.g., `ja` and `en`) are updated simultaneously. Do not wait for the user to prompt you to update the other languages.
